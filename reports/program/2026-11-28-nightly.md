appear in the diagnostics blocked list. The dashboard undercounts blocked tickers.

   per-ticker `{ic: float, n: int}` for the full blocked set.

   whitelist.

3. **`tests/unit/test_ic_block_diagnostic_parity.py`**: 7 unit tests verifying:
   - Tickers with IC in (-0.05, 0.0] now appear in `tickers_ic_blocked`
