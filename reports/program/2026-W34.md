## ADDENDUM — 2026-08-19: H14 independently verified, and a calibration caveat on PR #36

*Added post-publication after an owner-requested review of the exit path. Does not amend the
W34 figures above; extends them with Aug 17–18 fills (n=49 → n=68).*

### 1. H14 root cause is confirmed — and it is not a bug in the exit code

The exit ladder in `signal_loop.py:1900-1932` executes correctly on every bar and is evaluated
*before* `max_hold`. Independently checked across all 68 v0.6.0 trades: **0 of 68 ended beyond
any barrier**. Nothing was missed. The barriers are unreachable by construction.

The defect is a unit mismatch in *time*. `_atr_exits()` (`signal_loop.py:246-262`) derives
thresholds from **daily** sigma, but the trade now lives 30 bars — `sqrt(30/390)` = **0.277**
of a day's sigma. Restated against the move a 30-minute hold can actually make:

| Barrier | Intended | Effective vs a 30-bar hold |
|---------|----------|----------------------------|
| Stop `1.1σ` | 1.1σ | **3.97σ** |
| Trailing `1.2σ` | 1.2σ | **4.33σ** |
| Take-profit `1.5σ` | 1.5σ | **5.41σ** |

This corroborates PR #36's diagnosis and its `_HOLD_VOL_SCALE` fix at a larger sample
(n=68 vs n=31 in the PR body).

**Provenance:** the geometry was deliberately tuned on 2026-07-24 (v0.5.3) to be reachable
inside a **390-bar** hold — see the comment block at `signal_loop.py:118-131`, which still
describes that regime. v0.6.0 cut the hold 13× on Aug 2 and left the barriers untouched. The
decalibration was a silent side effect of the project's best change to date. **That comment
block must be rewritten in the same PR that merges H14, or it will mislead the next reader.**

The floors are the worse half. Calm names (AAPL, GS, XOM, PFE) clamp to the 0.5%
`DAILY_VOL_FLOOR`, pinning the stop at the 1.0% `SIZING_STOP_LOSS_FLOOR` against a 30-min
sigma near 0.14% — roughly a **7σ** stop. `CATASTROPHIC_STOP_MULT = 2.0` (`signal_loop.py:190`)
then doubles an already-dead stop, so the PDT emergency path is dead too.

### 2. NEW — PR #36 as written may overshoot into edge truncation

The W34 review flags that "the exit distribution will shift" but does not size the shift. It is
larger than it looks, and it cuts against the thesis of v0.6.0.

PR #36 rescales the stop to 1.1σ **of the hold window**. At current vols that is a median stop
distance of **0.723%**, against a median realized absolute move at exit of **0.601%** — a ratio
of 0.83. The stop would sit *inside* the distribution of ordinary trade wander.

Trades whose return was still past a given level **at exit** (a strict lower bound — excursions
that dipped through mid-hold and recovered are not counted, so the true fire rate is materially
higher):

| Stop, in σ of the 30-bar window | Trades past it at exit | % of book |
|---|---|---|
| **1.10σ ← PR #36 as written** | 11 | **16.2%** |
| 1.25σ | 8 | 11.8% |
| 1.50σ | 6 | 8.8% |
| 2.00σ – 3.00σ | 0 | 0.0% |
| 3.97σ (current, live) | 0 | 0.0% |

The risk: premature stops truncate a 15–30 minute edge before it realizes. That is the same
failure mode as the `signal_reversal` 4-bar truncation identified in the 2026-07-07
profitability diagnosis, and it is precisely what v0.6.0 was built to stop doing.

Against that risk, the measured upside is small. Across all 68 trades a correctly-scaled stop
at 1.5σ would have saved **+$76**; at 2.0σ and wider, nothing. The worst single loss in the
entire M2 window was **−$243 (−1.96%, SMCI, Aug 18)**. The 30-bar timer is already performing
the risk-control function, and performing it well.

**Program Office position:** merge H14 — the units are unambiguously wrong and leaving a
decorative stop in a risk system is indefensible under CLAUDE.md. But merge it as a **disaster
stop, not a trading stop**: a level near **2.5–3σ of the hold window** never fires in normal
conditions (0/68 here) while still capping a tail, and preserves the v0.6.0 thesis. The 1.1σ
setting in PR #36 is a strategy change wearing a bug-fix label — at a 16%+ fire rate it changes
what the bot *is*, and the freeze should apply to it. Recommend the desk re-run the H14
backtest at 1.1σ / 2.0σ / 2.5σ / 3.0σ before merge and pick on evidence.

Note also that under PR #36 `CATASTROPHIC_STOP_MULT = 2.0` becomes 2.2σ of the hold window —
i.e. **reachable for the first time**. The PDT emergency-exit path goes from dead code to live
behavior on merge. That should be a deliberate decision, not a side effect.

### 3. Real exposure, stated plainly

The tail is unhedged at the position level. At 12.5% notional × 6 slots, a halt, gap, or
single-name headline mid-hold is capped by nothing but the clock. Portfolio-level circuit
breakers remain the only backstop — which is what caught the Jul 28 drawdown. This is a
low-probability, high-severity gap, not a live P&L leak.
