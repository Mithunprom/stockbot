## PM Assessment — North Star Check

M2 is trending correctly: n grew from 16 to 49 in one week, PF sits at 2.33, net PnL is
+$1,748. But two facts should temper confidence. First, the sample remains noise — remove
MSTR and CIEN (the two biggest winners) and PF drops materially; the gate at n=100 exists
precisely to wait this out. Second, M2 has never had a functioning stop-loss (H14 unmerged).
Every trade in the sample exited via max_hold; we do not yet know what the bot looks like with
calibrated exits. When H14 merges, the exit distribution will shift and any trailing stats from
the pre-H14 window will need a footnote.

The path to the north star (stable ops → measured edge → paper gate → product) is unambiguous:
merge the structural fixes, keep the strategy freeze, let n→100 mature. Railway worker is the
only external dependency. At Week 9, it is not a technical problem — it is a ten-minute decision.
