entire project. Holding positions 26× past the model's training horizon (IC=0.004 at 390
bars) was the root cause of the −8% drawdown. The fix is research-grade: two OOS windows,
replicated holdout, cost-robust at 10× slippage. The retired M2 window (PF=0.636@n=35) does
not describe the new bot's expected behavior.

But the fix is still purely theoretical for the live ledger. Until the halt is lifted
and 100 trades close under v0.6.0, the north star metric (PF≥1.2) is unmeasured. The path:
lift halt → v0.6.0 starts trading → accumulate n=100 (~4–5 weeks at ~5 trades/day) →
measure. Every week of continued halt is a week of measurement delay.

The Railway worker bottleneck is now in its 6th consecutive week. The desk has been thorough
on research and code quality; the system architecture is sounder than it has ever been. But
zero hypothesis validations in 6 weeks while the validation-gate action sits at 10 minutes
is a genuine management failure. The Program Office flags this as the single highest-return
action available to the project.
