**Strategy question next:** the 30-bar max_hold with H14 stops unmerged has produced 98/98 max_hold
exits and is operating without its designed risk management. The true performance of the v0.6.0
strategy is untestable until H14 stops are live. This is the core PM objection to any gate call:
we are not measuring the intended strategy.

**M2 gate call:** With PF=0.926@n=98, the gate fails. The owner must decide whether to:
1. Continue the M2 window and run toward a larger n target, accepting that the gate may still not
   be met — requires Kelly recovery and a positive trading run.
2. Declare M2 incomplete and focus on fixing the structural defects (H14, H15, H24) before
   re-measuring, which would require a strategy change (merge H14) and a gate reset.

Option 2 is more honest and likely produces better long-run outcomes, but requires the owner to
formally reset the gate. Option 1 is defensible only if H14 stops are merged immediately.

**North star position:** We are regressing. M2 is not on track. M5 is not within a realistic quarter.
The only immediate value-unlock is the Railway worker — 12 weeks idle. The hypothesis debt is now
structurally stale (H4/H2 > 58 days blocked). The program needs a decision on whether to continue
the current measurement or restart it with the structural fixes in place.
